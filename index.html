About The Author

Hi, I'm H.A. Diyana, an aspiring author who believes stories should be as fun to read as they are to write. My books feature strong heroines, swoony heroes, and plenty of witty banter.

When I'm not writing, you can find me drinking too much coffee, browsing Pinterest, or daydreaming about my next story. I live in Bangladesh with my family and sarcasm.

My mission is to create novels that feel like a warm hug, thrilling adventure and leave you wondering long after you've turned the last page.
